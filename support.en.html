Frequently asked questions

How do I add my first address?

1. On the home screen, tap the ellipsis (•••) under the ATEQO logo at the top to open the panel, and set your starting point (your depot or your home): type the address, pick a Google suggestion, confirm.

2. In the add bar, type a delivery address, dictate it by voice, or tap the scanner icon to photograph a parcel label. The app detects the address automatically.

3. On the map, tap the grey marker of the new stop to assign it a sector (1 to 5) and confirm.

The app says "No route calculated". What should I do?

Two conditions are required to run the optimization:

  • Having set a starting point in the Base tab
  • Having at least one confirmed address (with a sector assigned, not a grey marker)

Once both conditions are met, the "Optimize route" button becomes active. You can launch it from the "My route" tab or directly from the "Route" tab.

The label scan does not detect the address correctly

OCR works best when:

  • The label is sharp and well lit
  • You frame only the address area (not the whole parcel)
  • The text is readable to the eye (not creased, not too small)

If the detection gets it wrong, you can manually correct the number and the street in the scan dialog before confirming. The postcode is remembered between scans to make entry easier.

If the app displays "OCR service not configured": this is a server-side problem, contact support.

GPS location is not working

First check that permission is granted: Settings → Apps → ATEQO → Permissions → Location → enable "While using the app".

ATEQO only tracks your position during active navigation. It never uses your position in the background.

If you switch to external Google Maps during navigation, the position may be suspended by the system after around 30 seconds. This is normal — coming back to ATEQO reactivates tracking.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen (Account tab):

  1. Click "Forgot password?" below the form
  2. Enter your email address
  3. You will receive an email with a reset link
  4. Click the link and choose a new password

If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder. The sender is noreply@mail.app.supabase.io.

I am Premium but my free-tier limits are still there

Premium status is synchronized when the app opens and right after a purchase. If the app has not recognized your subscription:

  1. Sign out then sign back in → forces a re-check
  2. Close and fully reopen the app
  3. If the problem persists after your purchase, contact support with your account email

You can also check your subscription status by clicking "Manage my subscription" in the Account tab — this opens the Google Play Subscriptions page with your billing history.

How do I cancel my Premium subscription?

Everything goes through Google Play, reachable from the app:

  1. Account tab → click "Manage subscription"
  2. You are redirected to the Google Play Subscriptions page
  3. Select ATEQO then click "Cancel subscription"
  4. Confirm

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period paid for (you keep Premium until that date). No penalty, no commitment.

How do I delete my account and all my data?

Send an email to cargo.app.admin@gmail.com from the address linked to your ATEQO account, with the subject "Account deletion".

In accordance with the GDPR (Article 17, right to erasure), we will carry out the deletion within one month:

  • Account data (email, username, Supabase identifier)
  • Deletion of your subscription data (RevenueCat)
  • A Google Play subscription that is still active must be cancelled by you through Google Play (Account → Subscriptions) — Google handles payments

Data stored locally on your device (routes, history) is erased automatically when the app is uninstalled.

More details about your rights in the privacy policy.

What are the differences between Free and Premium?

On sign-up: your first 7 routes come with no limit at all, with no payment method to enter.

Free plan (afterwards):

  • 15 addresses maximum per route
  • 3 active sectors (S1, S2, S3)
  • Unlimited optimizations
  • All other features (scan, GPS navigation, loading checklist, proximity alerts) are included

Premium:

  • Unlimited addresses
  • 5 sectors (S1 to S5)
  • Address book (saved recurring addresses)

The app shows no advertising, on any plan.

Does the app work offline?

Partly. Here is what works without internet:

  • The app and its interface
  • Viewing the addresses you have already entered
  • GPS (your phone's sensor)
  • History of saved routes

What requires internet:

  • Google Maps maps (loading the tiles)
  • Google address suggestions and geocoding
  • Route optimization (uses the Google Routes API)
  • Sign-in / Premium subscription verification
  • Label OCR scan (Google Cloud Vision)

During a route, if you enter an area with no 4G/5G, navigation that has already been calculated keeps working (local GPS does not need internet to operate).

Which Android versions does the app support? And iOS?

ATEQO is currently available on Android only, through Google Play. Android: 7.0 minimum (Nougat, released in 2016).

On very old models, some features may be slower (in particular the parcel label OCR, which needs a recent camera). GPS navigation and route optimization work everywhere.

An iOS version is being considered but is not available yet.

Report a bug

If you run into unexpected behaviour:

  1. Note the app version (shown in Account → About)
  2. Note your phone model and OS version
  3. Describe what happened and what you expected instead
  4. Send it all to cargo.app.admin@gmail.com

A screenshot or a video is very welcome if you have one.
